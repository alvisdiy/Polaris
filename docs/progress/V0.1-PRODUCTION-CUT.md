# Polaris V0.1 Production Cut

Milestone report. Every number here was measured with the scripts named beside
it; nothing is estimated.

---

## 1. Starting State

Measured with `scripts/m11_baseline.py` before any V0.1 change:

```text
records            176   (approved 23, pending_review 7, rejected 145, needs_correction 1)
providers          225
sources            205
raw documents      319
versions           305
review actions     175
frontier rows    4,760
approved count      23   (target 30–50)
```

Three defects were inherited from POST-M10 and named as production blockers:
the `"Unknown"` sentinel in `api/routers/scholarships.py`,
`RecommendationListResponse.total` capped at the page length, and inherited
repository-wide Ruff debt. All three are addressed in §4 and §11.

M11's page-intent classifier existed but ran only in `scripts/m11_discovery_run.py`.
The production acquisition path, `AcquisitionService.acquire_from_source`, never
called it — so the index-page failure POST-M10 measured was still reachable in
production. §4 closes that.

## 2. Production Scope

V0.1 ships the journey in §2 of the brief and nothing else. Nothing was built
that the journey does not need.

```text
browse / search / filter  ->  detail  ->  official source  ->  profile
        ->  deterministic matches  ->  strengths & gaps  ->  compare
        ->  shortlist (localStorage)  ->  official application page
```

Deliberately **not** built: accounts, authentication, chatbot, autonomous
research, notifications, email, mobile, microservices, a new orchestration
framework, a new database or matching architecture. The matching weights
(`Country 20 / Degree 20 / Field 20 / GPA 15 / Language 10 / Funding 5 /
Research 5 / Work 3 / Leadership 2`) are byte-for-byte unchanged; §6 shows the
verification.

## 3. Data Catalogue

Measured with `scripts/v01_snapshot.py` after the final curation pass.

```text
Approved:            28
Pending:              0
Rejected:           147
Needs correction:     1
Countries:            9   (UK 7, Germany 5, Singapore 5, Japan 4, Indonesia 3,
                          Australia 1, China 1, France 1, United States 1)
Providers:           12
Degree:      Master 15, not stated 6, PhD 3, Bachelor 2, Exchange 1, Short Course 1
Funding:  Fully Funded 18, not stated 9, Partial Funded 1
```

Every approved record has an official URL (28/28), 20 carry a deadline, 28 carry
an application URL, 8 carry a `last_verified_at`.

**The 30–50 target was not reached. 28 are approved, two short.** The cause is
measured, not guessed:

- The pending queue held **7 records**. All 7 were read individually against
  their captures; 5 were approved and 2 rejected. Nothing else was waiting.
- The remaining pool is 147 `rejected` rows. Of those, **47 were rejected for
  "no field provenance" alone** — records whose name came from a document the
  record cannot name. They cannot be approved without either re-acquiring the
  page through the normal pipeline (which §25 of the brief forbids doing in
  bulk) or inventing a `document_id` (which is fabricated provenance). The rest
  fail the V0.1 identity rules: `'Grant Benefits\nGrant'`, `'Unknown
  Scholarship'`, `'Sport Scholarship'`, `'Guide to Scholarship'`.

Per §24 this was treated as a documented constraint rather than a reason to
keep crawling. §3 of the brief's DoD allows exactly this outcome.

### 3.1 How the 7 pending records were decided

`scripts/v01_curate.py` applied mechanical rules. The judgement calls — "is this
one opportunity", and "is this value supported by the page" — are in
`scripts/v01_final_curation.py`, where every decision is written by hand next
to the `document_id` it was read from, and applied through `ReviewService`:

| Record | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| SP Scholarship & SP Engineering Scholarship | approve | one award, one page, eligibility list present |
| SP Undergraduate Scholarship | **correct then approve** | `funding_type` corrected `Fully Funded` → NULL |
| PFP Study Award | approve | award quantum and eligibility stated |
| NTU Opportunity Grant (NOG) | approve | states "up to S$10,000 over the course of up to four years" |
| Women in Science Bursary | approve | states £1000/annum, who it is for, how it is awarded |
| Overseas Student Programme Loan/ Travel Award | reject | two instruments in one record; a loan is not a scholarship |
| Financial Aid for Overseas Programmes | reject | a section describing three aids, naming no award |

**The correction is the one that mattered.** The extractor had mapped the
phrase *"Full scholarship award of $30,000 will be given out in three tranches
of $10,000 each"* to `Fully Funded`. A fixed cash award is not a statement that
all costs are covered, and §16 forbids an unsupported "Fully Funded". The
field is now NULL with the source wording attached in the provenance and the
reason on the record. Approving it as extracted would have shipped a wrong
funding claim to users.

The five approvals each carry a **recorded quality override on `degree`**. The
publication gate wants a degree level; these pages state eligibility without
naming one ("full-time diploma course students", "Year 1 undergraduate
freshman", "Enrolled PFP students"). Mapping `undergraduate` or `diploma` to
`Bachelor` is precisely the decision ADR-018 declined to make on evidence, so
the field stays NULL and the override reason quotes the wording that caused it.
The override is recorded on both the version row and the audit row — this is
the mechanism `ReviewService.approve(override_quality=...)` exists for, not a
bypass.

## 4. API Changes

### 4.1 Defect A — the `"Unknown"` sentinel is gone

`api/routers/scholarships.py` substituted the literal string `"Unknown"` for a
missing provider or country. A client could not tell a missing value from a
real provider called "Unknown", and a filter on `country=Unknown` would have
returned records.

The smallest correct contract is **null**. `provider_name` and `country` are now
`Optional[str]` and are `None` when the relation is absent. `api/schemas.py`,
`frontend/src/api/types.ts` and `frontend/src/components/ScholarshipCard.vue`
were updated together — the TypeScript type is `string | null` and the card
renders nothing rather than the word "Unknown". No compatibility shim preserves
the old value. Verified live: the smoke test asserts no sentinel in the list or
on the detail of any record.

### 4.2 Defect B — `total` now means total

`RecommendationListResponse.total` was derived from the top-10 result list, so
`total` was capped at the page size and a truncated page looked complete. The
contract is now:

```text
total    — every approved, non-expired scholarship that matched the profile
returned — how many are in `results`, capped at the page size
```

The count moved into `services/matching/ranking/engine.py` (which sees the full
candidate set) and is carried through `orchestrator.py` and `service.py`.
Measured live: a profile matching the UK catalogue returns **`total=27`,
`returned=10`** — before, `total` would have said 10.

### 4.3 Canonical paths

`POST /api/v1/recommendations` is canonical. `POST /api/v1/matching` remains as
the legacy path POST-M10 left behind; it returns an identical payload, which is
the compatibility requirement that keeps it — removing it would break the
deployed client for no gain.

**The frontend was calling the legacy path.** `frontend/src/api/client.ts`
posted to `${API_BASE}/matching`. Because both routes return the same body,
nothing broke and every test passed while the client sat on the non-canonical
path — the exact failure §8 of the brief warns about. It is now
`${API_BASE}/recommendations`, and `frontend/src/tests/client.test.ts` asserts
the URL so the drift cannot come back silently. Verified in the built bundle:
`recommendations` present, `matching` absent.

### 4.4 Page-intent wiring (M11 §6, minimum only)

`AcquisitionService.acquire_from_source` now classifies **every fetched page**
with `services/exploration/page_intent.classify_page_intent` and skips
extraction when the verdict is anything other than `SCHOLARSHIP_DETAIL`.
`AcquisitionMetrics` gains `page_intent_counts` and `pages_skipped_not_detail`
so the funnel is measurable in production, not only in the M11 script.

Two deliberate limits:

- **`UNKNOWN` is still extracted.** It means the classifier found no evidence
  either way, which is not the same as evidence that the page is a list.
  Discarding it would throw away real opportunities on a signal that is silent
  rather than negative; the review queue is the safety net.
- **The discovery architecture was not redesigned.** Index pages are still
  discovery surfaces in `scripts/m11_discovery_run.py`; the production path
  simply stopped pretending they were scholarships.

Wiring this exposed a real classifier defect, fixed on evidence. The M9
acquisition fixture (`/scholarship/xyz-2027/`, one award named, one deadline
sentence) was classified `SCHOLARSHIP_OVERVIEW` and therefore never extracted.
Measuring 319 stored pages showed why: `"application period"` or
`"application window"` appears in **47 of them**, and the deadline vocabulary
knew only "applications close", so **31 documents were invisible** as deadline
evidence. Three phrasings were added to `_DEADLINE_LANGUAGE`, and a
single-signal DETAIL branch was allowed when the claim is specific (a title
naming one award + a year in the path + a non-collection slug). Before the
change: 1 of 6 golden DETAIL pages at 2 signal families, index pages correctly
held. After: **all 6 DETAIL, all 5 INDEX and the 1 OVERVIEW classify exactly as
labelled** — `tests/integration/test_discovery_intent.py`.

## 5. Frontend Changes

Only what the contract changes forced:

- `frontend/src/api/client.ts` — matching now posts to the canonical
  `/api/v1/recommendations` (see §4.3).
- `frontend/src/api/types.ts` — `provider_name`/`country` are `string | null` on
  the list, detail and matched-result shapes.
- `frontend/src/components/ScholarshipCard.vue` — builds its provider/country
  line from `[provider_name, country].filter(Boolean)`, so a null renders as
  nothing rather than the sentinel.
- `frontend/src/tests/client.test.ts` — regression test for the canonical path.
- `vite.config.ts` proxy fixed and a `test` script added; `npm test` had never
  run (its `include` glob was wrong relative to `root: 'src'`). It now runs
  **20 tests** and is part of the definition of done.

No component was redesigned. Search, filters, detail, profile, matching,
compare and shortlist already existed from M9 and are verified working in §10.

## 6. Matching Verification

Weights unchanged: `Country 20 / Degree 20 / Field 20 / GPA 15 / Language 10 /
Funding 5 / Research 5 / Work 3 / Leadership 2`. The scoring formula,
`FilterEngine`, `RankingEngine` semantics and the publication predicate were not
touched. The M11 Australia ranking observation is **not** treated as evidence
for changing anything.

Verified through the live API in `scripts/v01_smoke.py`:

```text
scores within 0-100                     ok  (min 73.0, max 95.0)
scores ordered descending                ok
every result has strengths and gaps      ok
every result links to a source           ok
total >= returned                        ok  (27 >= 10)
```

**Known consequence, reported rather than tuned away:** the degree criterion
carries 20 of 100 points and `_score_degree` compares the profile's degree to
`scholarship.degree`. Six approved records publish `degree = NULL` (§3.1), so
they score 0 on that criterion for every user. Fixing this means either
changing the scoring formula (forbidden by §13) or widening the degree
vocabulary (ADR-018). It is left visible rather than papered over.

## 8. Security Regression

M10 security was verified, not redesigned. Nothing was disabled: SafeFetch, SSRF
validation, redirect validation, robots and transient retries are unchanged, and
the full suite (1297 tests, §11) includes the M10 security regression tests,
which pass.

Two real bypasses found during M11 remain fixed and are re-verified by the suite
in the files named:

- `GenericPlaywrightConnector.discover()` navigated without a robots check and
  without URL validation — the one path that could read a page `fetch()` had
  refused.
- `GenericHTTPConnector.discover()` called `self.client.get()` directly,
  bypassing `SafeFetch` entirely, and returned `[entry_url]` on failure, so an
  index page reached the extractor as if it were a scholarship link.

Secrets: `.env` is untracked and uncommitted; `.env.example` was updated to
document the new configuration keys without values (`DATABASE_PASSWORD` is the
literal placeholder `replace_me`). CORS is **off by default** and enabled only
from `POLARIS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS` — it is no longer a wildcard.

## 9. Deployment Configuration

```text
DATABASE_URL             postgres, from the environment only
POLARIS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS  empty by default; CORS middleware not added when empty
VITE_API_BASE_URL        frontend base URL, set at build time
start command            uvicorn api.app:app
health                   GET /health -> {"status":"ok","database":"ok"}
```

`/health` used to return `ok` without touching the database, so an orchestrator
would keep a container alive that could not serve a single request. It now
opens a connection and returns `degraded` with HTTP 503 when the database is
unreachable. Verified live: `{'status': 'ok', 'database': 'ok'}`.

Migrations are reproducible: `alembic head` = `d5e6f7a8b9c0`, **15 migration
files, 0 added by this milestone**. The schema did not change because no
production blocker required one.

## 10. End-to-End Smoke Test

`scripts/v01_smoke.py` runs the journey against `uvicorn api.app:app` — the same
app object the deployment serves, with nothing mocked and nothing written.

```text
  [ok] health returns ok — {'status': 'ok', 'database': 'ok'}
  [ok] list returns 200 / catalogue populated — 28 approved / no sentinel
  [ok] search 200; no-match search is an empty page, not an error
  [ok] country and degree filters 200
  [ok] detail 200; official source present; last_verified_at exposed
  [ok] unknown id 404; malformed id 404 not 500; limit=0 422; limit=99999 422
  [ok] incomplete profile 422 with field messages
  [ok] matching 200; total=27 returned=10; scores 73.0–95.0 ordered
  [ok] canonical /api/v1/recommendations returns the identical contract
  [ok] frontend served at /, /discover, /match (production build)
SMOKE TEST PASSED
```

Edge cases covered: empty search, no-match search, incomplete profile, valid
profile, records with sparse optional fields, unknown id, malformed id,
out-of-range pagination. **One case from the brief is not covered: an expired
scholarship.** The catalogue contains no record whose deadline has passed with
`deadline_state` still open, so there was nothing to request. This is a gap in
the evidence, not a claim that the case works.

### Performance (measured, 1 warm-up pass then 5 samples, two runs)

```text
                              run 1 median   run 2 median   worst max (either run)
/api/v1/scholarships?limit=20      11.5 ms          28.4 ms           32.0 ms
/api/v1/scholarships?limit=100     29.7 ms          10.8 ms           36.9 ms
/api/v1/matching                   16.9 ms          31.6 ms           40.8 ms
/api/v1/recommendations            16.4 ms          14.9 ms           40.8 ms
```

The spread between runs is larger than the spread within them, which is what a
28-row catalogue on a shared development machine looks like: nothing here is
bottlenecked, and the medians are not precise to better than about 10 ms. The
claim worth making is "all four are tens of milliseconds", not a specific
figure.

Frontend bundle: 116.96 kB main chunk (45.36 kB gzipped). No optimisation was
performed because no bottleneck was found; §19 forbids premature optimisation
and nothing measured justified it. **Initial-load time was not measured** —
see §13.

## 12. Database Integrity

`scripts/m10_integrity_audit.py` → **2 findings, both pre-existing and both
unchanged by this milestone**:

1. **`no placeholder scholarship name: 17`** — the M8 `'Unknown Scholarship'`
   rows. All 17 are rejected and none is public.
2. **`every approved record has an approval action: 19`** — the legacy
   bootstrap seed predates `ReviewService` and carries no review action, no
   version and no field provenance.

Finding 2 is the one that matters for a production cut, and it is not closed:
**19 of the 28 published records have no recorded reviewer.** They were
approved by `scripts/seed_manual_scholarships.py` writing
`extraction_status` directly. Re-approving them would mean recording a review
action for work this milestone did not do, so it was not done. The honest
consequences are visible in §7: those 19 records carry `Fully Funded` with no
source wording and `field_of_study` lists with no provenance. They are
documented, not fixed.

No fabricated history was added to make the audit look clean.

## 13. Known Limitations

1. **28 approved, target was 30–50.** Measured cause in §3.
2. **19 of 28 published records have no review action and no field provenance.**
   The largest gap between what V0.1 claims and what it can evidence.
3. **19 records publish `Fully Funded` with no recorded source wording.** Same
   records. Whether each claim is correct was not re-verified; what is missing
   is the evidence, not necessarily the value.
4. **Six records publish `degree = NULL`** and therefore score 0 on a
   20-point criterion for every user (§6).
5. **The expired-scholarship path is untested** because the catalogue contains
   no expired-but-open record (§10).
6. **The journey was not clicked through in a real browser.** The smoke test
   drives the HTTP API and confirms the production build is served at `/`,
   `/discover` and `/match`; it does not render a page, so client-side routing,
   hydration and the localStorage shortlist round-trip are covered by unit tests
   and by "the bundle builds", not by an observed user session. §18 of the
   brief asks for a desktop browser and that was not done.
7. **Frontend initial-load time was not measured**, only bundle size (§10).
8. **`last_verified_at` is populated on 8 of 28 records.** Nothing currently
   refreshes it for re-crawled records.
9. **172 inherited Ruff findings** remain repo-wide by decision, not oversight.
10. **`evidence.page_signals` is still written by nobody.** The column exists;
    the M11 verdict is counted in `AcquisitionMetrics` instead. Not required for
    V0.1 and not fixed.

## 14. Deferred M11 / M12 Work

- **Duplicate identity is too weak.** A name carrying a subtitle escapes the
  `provider + name + country` key and produced two "Sanctuary Scholarships" rows
  in M11. Stopped under M11 §27 (ambiguous duplicate identity) rather than
  guessed at. Still the highest-value correctness fix after this milestone.
- **Index-page link following in production.** The production path now refuses
  to extract an index page but does not *follow* it. Following links from
  official surfaces lives only in `scripts/m11_discovery_run.py`.
- **`evidence.page_signals`** should be written by the acquisition path.
- **DAAD and the Japanese `/types/` surface** yielded zero detail pages in the
  M11 experiment; both need a discovery approach this milestone did not build.
- **Re-acquiring the 47 provenance-less rejected records** is the only measured
  route to more approved records without inventing provenance.

## 16. Final Status

# PARTIAL

Not BLOCKED: everything shippable is shipped and verified — the API contract is
coherent, the publication boundary holds, the journey works end to end against
a production-like server, the full suite is green, coverage is above the gate
and no M10 security guarantee was weakened.

Not READY FOR PRODUCTION, for two reasons that are about the data rather than
the code:

1. **19 of the 28 records a visitor can see were never reviewed.** A product
   whose headline promise is trustworthiness cannot lead with records whose
   publication has no reviewer behind it, and whose `Fully Funded` claim has no
   recorded source wording. Fixing this means re-reading those 19 official
   pages and recording the outcome — work this milestone did not do, and did
   not fake.
2. **The catalogue is two records short of the target**, for the measured
   reason in §3, and the only honest route to more records — re-acquisition
   through the normal pipeline — was out of scope by §24 and §25.

Both are curation work against a working system, not architecture. The system
is ready for that work; it is not ready to be trusted with 28 records as though
all 28 had been checked.
