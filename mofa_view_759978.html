[KF] 2025-2026 Scholarship for Graduate Studies & Fellowship for Postdoctoral Research

DATE
2025-08-06

The Korea Foundation(KF) has been offering scholarships and research support to foreign graduate students and postdoctoral researchers specializing in Korea-related fields. Through these scholarship and fellowship programs, the KF nurtures the next generation of Korean studies scholars while promoting the activities of Korea specialists worldwide. 


O Application Submission: 2025.7.21. ~ 2025.9.12. 18:00 KST


O Apply: KF online application system (http://apply.kf.or.kr)


O Inquiries: KF Fellowships Department (scholarship@kf.or.kr)
